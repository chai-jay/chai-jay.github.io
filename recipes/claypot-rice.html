Claypot Rice

Ingredients

  • 10 oz Jasmine Rice (uncooked)
  • 2 bone-in skin-on chicken leg quarter
  • 4 dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 2 Chinese sausages
  • 1 shallot
  • 1 tbsp ginger
  • 2 scallions
  • 4 tbsp oil - 1 for pre-cooked rice, 1 for chicken (right before cooking it), and 2 for toasting the rice
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil (for chicken - right before cooking it)

  • Chicken Marinade:

    • 1 tbsp soy sauce
    • 2 tbsp oyster sauce
    • 0.5 tsp salt
    • 1 tsp sugar
    • 1 tbsp cornstarch
    • 3 tbsp water
    • 1 tsp Chinese cooking wine
    • 0.25 tsp white pepper

  • Serving Sauce:

    • 1 tbsp oil
    • 1 scallion - cut to 1-inch segments
    • 1 shallot - thinly sliced
    • 1/2 bunch of cilantro - cut into 1-inch segments (about 1/3 cup)
    • 4 cloves of garlic - smashed
    • 1 tbsp ginger - sliced thinly
    • 2 tbsp Chinese cooking wine
    • 1/3 cup water
    • 1/3 cup light soy sauce
    • 1/3 cup dark soy sauce
    • 2 tbsp sugar

  • Mushroom Marinade:

    • 0.5 tbsp oyster sauce
    • 1 tsp sugar
    • 0.5 tsp cornstarch

Prep Steps

  1. Wash rice and add water to cover. Let it soak for 1 to 3 hrs for shorter and more even cook. Break a rice grain in half and there should be no solid white in the center.
  2. Soak shiitake mushrooms in water for 30 minutes to 8hrs.
  3. Soak Chinese sausage links in water for 2 hrs.
  4. Prep the chicken for marinading. Mince the shallot and ginger. Cut the chicken into 1-inch to 2-inch pieces. Wash after to remove any potential bone shards. Pat dry.
  5. In a bowl, combine the chicken marinade ingredients together and mix. Then add the chicken, minced shallot, and minced ginger to the bowl and massage the chicken to evenly coat each piece. Leave in fridge to marinade for 30 minutes to 8 hrs.
  6. Prep sauce vegetables ingredients. Heat oil and cook vegetables ingredients on medium-low heat for 2 minutes until fragrant and slightly golden. Add rest of the ingredients. Bring to boil over high heat and reduce heat to low and simmer for 7 minutes until thickened. It should be slightly thicker than soy sauce. Strain the solid ingredients out and save the sauce for later in fridge.
  7. Prep the mushrooms for marinading. Cut off mushroom stems (bc they're too hard) and cut the mushroom caps into slices. In a bowl, combine the mushroom marinade ingredients together and mix. Then add the sliced mushrooms and mix until evenly coated.
  8. Dice the scallions and save for garnish later.
  9. Slice the Chinese sausages on a bias into 1/4 inch thick pieces.

Cooking Steps

  1. Bring enough water to cover rice to a boil.
  2. Heat a claypot on medium heat for 1-2 minutes until hot. Never use high heat!
  3. Drain the rice and add to claypot. Add 1 tbsp oil to the rice and mix well.
  4. Add enough boiling water to cover the rice by about 1/4 inch. Cover with lid. Let it heat up until water starts to boil or steam starts to come out. While waiting, prep the toppings in the next step.
  5. Add 1 tbsp oil and 1 tbsp sesame oil to the marinaded chicken and mix. Get the other toppings ready to go into the claypot.
  6. Once the water starts to boil, uncover the lid and let the rice cook uncovered until the water is below the rice. Poke ventilation holes all over the rice.
  7. Add the chicken pieces and Chinese sausage slices on top of the rice. Spread them out evenly. Then, add mushrooms on top. Cover with lid and turn the heat down to low and cook for 10 minutes or until you can hear consistent sizzling crackle sound. Inconsistent loud pops means the heat is too high.
  8. Once crackling starts, drizzle 2 tbsp of oil around the edge of the closed lid so it can flow down into the rice.
  9. Then turn the heat up to medium again and with oven mitts or dry towels, tilt the pot so the flame is touching towards the side of the pot. Cook each quadrant of the pot's bottom for 1-2 minutes before moving to rotating to another quadrant. Repeat until the sizzling sound stops and the escaping steam slows to thin wisps of smoke. It should take about 10 minutes or so. If you smell smoke or a burning smell, remove from heat immediately. Toasty smell is okay.
  10. Remove the claypot from the heat and let it rest for 2 to 5 minutes with the lid on.
  11. Remove the lid and top with diced scallions and drizzle with desired amount of the prepared serving sauce. Mix everything together and serve.
