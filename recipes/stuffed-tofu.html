Hakka Stuffed Tofu

Ingredients

  • One (?) container of firm tofu
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil

  • Meat Filling:

    • 8 oz pork loin
    • 3 garlic cloves
    • 4 oz shrimp
    • A little bit of dried shrimp/prawn (Xia Mi 虾米)
    • 1 oz jicama (Sah Koh in Cantonese 沙葛)
    • (Optional) Dash of cumin seeds

Prep Steps

  1. Day before cooking, do these prep steps!
  2. Take the tofu out of the container (but save the container). Drain the liquid off and cut the tofu block into desired size. Mom likes about 1in x 2.5in.
  3. Gently rub salt all over the tofu pieces. Put the salted tofu pieces back into the tofu container. Leave in fridge overnight.
  4. Chop up all the filling ingredients (including the pork loin until it's almost a meat-paste consistency) and mix together in a bowl/container until evenly mixed. Leave covered in fridge overnight.
  5. Wait until the next day. The tofu should have firmed up a little.
  6. Cut a slit into the "top" of each tofu piece.
  7. Stuff the meat filling into the slits with a spoon.

Cooking Steps

  1. Note, there is a fried version of this where you just heat up frying oil and fry until the meat filling reaches 165 degrees F. But we will do a healthier braised version here.
  2. In a pan, heat up cooking oil with medium heat.
  3. Place the stuffed tofu pieces meat-side down and cook for about 4 minutes or until there is a brown crust on the top.
  4. Then cook the remaining 5 sides of the tofu pieces, about 1 minutes per side to slightly brown them.
  5. Put the tofu pieces into a steamer for about 15 minutes. Or until the internal temperature of the meat filling is 165 degrees F.
  6. Serve with whole pieces of green leaf lettuce to use as wraps for the tofu pieces.
