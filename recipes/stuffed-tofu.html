Hakka Stuffed Tofu

Ingredients

  • 3 containers of House Tofu brand's firm tofu (it must be this brand and firmness)
  • Salt
  • Cooking oil

  • Meat Filling:

    • 10 oz pork loin
    • 1/4 tsp salt
    • 1/2 tsp sugar
    • 1/2 tsp dark soy sauce
    • 1/2 tsp regular soy sauce
    • 1/2 tsp sweet soy sauce
    • 1/2 tsp Oyster sauce
    • Dash of white pepper
    • --
    • 1 tsp corn starch
    • 1/2 tsp sesame oil
    • 1 garlic clove
    • 1 small shallot
    • 1/4 of one jicama (Sah Koh in Cantonese 沙葛)
    • 2 large shrimp
    • --
    • 1 egg
    • 1/2 scallion
    • (Optional) Dash of cumin seeds

Prep Steps

  1. Day before cooking, do these prep steps!

  2. Tofu Prep Steps

    1. Drain each container of tofu.
    2. Take one tofu block out.
    3. Rinse all sides under tap water.
    4. Dust the top and bottom of the tofu block with salt.
    5. Rub the salt all over each of the 6 sides gently.
    6. Place on a colander.
    7. Repeat for every tofu block.
    8. Place colander in the fridge overnight to slightly dehydrate and season.

  3. Meat Filling Prep Steps

    1. Chop up the jicama, shallot, garlic and set aside. Give the jicama a good chop because it helps to give the meat filling a softer and better texture.
    2. Cut up each shrimp roughly (like into 4 or 5 pieces) and set aside. We still want to have shrimp chunks in the meat filling.
    3. Chop up pork loin until it's pork mince. We're looking for a ground-meat consistency, like we're making meatballs.
    4. In a container, add the pork mince, salt, sugar, dark soy sauce, regular soy sauce, sweet soy sauce, oyster sauce, and white pepper.
    5. Keep mixing and folding the meat with your hands until evenly mixed. We're looking to season the meat with the salt throughout before the next step.
    6. Add corn starch, sesame oil, garlic, shallots, jicama, and shrimp. Do another round of mixing/folding.
    7. Leave in the fridge covered overnight.
  4. The next day, do these next steps
  5. In the meat filling, add cumin seeds, 1 egg, 1/2 scallion chopped up. Mix/fold again until evenly mixed.
  6. Cut each tofu block in half. Either cut lengthwise or diagonally from corner-to-corner is fine (外婆 made it with both cuts).
  7. Cut a slit into the new "top" side of each tofu piece.
  8. Stuff the meat filling into the slits.

Cooking Steps

  1. Note, there is a fried version of this where you just heat up frying oil and fry until the meat filling reaches 165 degrees F. But we will do a healthier braised version here.
  2. In a pan, heat up cooking oil with medium heat.
  3. Place the stuffed tofu pieces meat-side down and cook for about 4 minutes or until there is a brown crust on the top.
  4. Then cook the remaining 5 sides of the tofu pieces, about 1 minutes per side to slightly brown them.
  5. Put the tofu pieces into a steamer for about 15 minutes. Or until the internal temperature of the meat filling is 165 degrees F.
  6. Serve with whole pieces of green leaf lettuce to use as wraps for the tofu pieces.
