Vegetable Stir Fry

Ingredients

  • Any green leafy vegetables (household favorites are Yu Choy, Gai Lan/Chinese Broccoli, and Chinese Cabbage)
  • A little bit of scallion/green onion
  • A little bit of dried shrimp/prawn (Xia Mi 虾米)
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 shallot
  • Salt to taste
  • 0.5-1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • (Optional) 0.5-1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • (Optional) 0.5-1 tbsp ground bean sauce
  • 1-3 tbsp cooking oil

Steps

  1. Wash vegetables. Make sure there's no sand in them!
  2. If necessary, cut the vegetables to desired size. The stems should not be too thick - if they are, slice them diagonally to expose their fleshy middle parts.
  3. Chop up scallion (only need a bit of the green tips and white parts), dried shrimp, garlic, and shallot. Adjust amount to taste.
  4. In a pan, heat up the cooking oil over medium heat.
  5. Add the chopped scallion, dried shrimp, garlic, and shallot to the oil. Stir fry for about 30-60 seconds or until fragrant.
  6. Add the vegetables, followed by 2-4 tbsp water, and then cover with a lid for 1-2 minutes to let the veggies steam. Watch for oil splatter.
  7. Uncover the lid and flip, stir fry, and toss the vegetables around to get them covered in the oil.
  8. If needed, cover with lid again for 1-2 minutes to steam/cook further.
  9. Add salt to taste. If adding sauces, add them now. Stir fry for another 1-2 minutes until the vegetables are coated. Then serve.
