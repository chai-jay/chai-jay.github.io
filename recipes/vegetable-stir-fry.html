Vegetable Stir Fry

Ingredients

  • Any green leafy vegetables (household favorites are Yu Choy, Gai Lan/Chinese Broccoli, and Chinese Cabbage)
  • A little bit of scallion/green onion
  • A little bit of dried shrimp/prawn (Xia Mi 虾米)
  • 1 garlic clove
  • Salt to taste
  • (Optional) 0.5-1 tbsp oyster sauce
  • (Optional) 0.5-1 tbsp light soy sauce
  • (Optional) 0.5-1 tbsp ground bean sauce
  • 1-2 tbsp cooking oil

Steps

  1. Wash vegetables. Make sure there's no sand in them!
  2. If necessary, cut the vegetables to desired size. The stems should not be too thick - if they are, slice them diagonally to expose their fleshy middle parts.
  3. Chop up scallion (only need a bit of the green tips and white parts), dried shrimp, and garlic. Adjust amount to taste.
  4. In a pan with a lid, heat up the cooking oil over medium heat.
  5. Add the chopped scallion, dried shrimp, and garlic to the oil. Stir fry for about 20 seconds or until fragrant.
  6. Add the vegetables and stir fry the vegetables around in the oil to get them covered in the oil.
  7. Add 1-2 tbsps of water and immediately cover with a lid to steam/wilt the vegetables. Careful of the splatter. Keep the lid on between 1-2 minutes (depending on how cooked you want the vegetables).
  8. If necessary, flip the vegetables over and cover with lid for another minute for an even cook.
  9. Add salt to taste. If adding optional sauces, add them now. Turn heat to low if needed. Stir fry for 30 seconds to 1 minute until the vegetables are coated. Then serve.
